# Experimental data: Walia, Carter et al. (2024)

`data.py` and `length_measurements.csv` are copied verbatim from the analysis
package the authors published alongside

> A. Walia, R. Carter, ... (2024) "Dynamic cell wall anisotropy governs
> differential growth during apical hook development in Arabidopsis."
> *Developmental Cell* 59:3245-3259.

Source: Zenodo record 13379829, directory `hook_model/`.

They are vendored here so `expdata.py` and `plot_compare.py` reproduce the
published measurements from the authors' own numbers rather than from values
transcribed by hand. Nothing in this directory is modified; set `WALIA_PKG`
to point at a different copy of the package if you have one.
